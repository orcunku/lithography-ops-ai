# Coolant flow interlock troubleshooting

Subsystem: cooling
Document ID: KB-COOL-04

A coolant flow interlock trip halts exposure to protect the system. First verify the flow sensor reading against a manual gauge to rule out a faulty sensor. If flow is genuinely low, check for a clogged filter, a failing pump, or a closed isolation valve. Do not bypass the interlock; restore flow and clear the alarm through the normal reset path.

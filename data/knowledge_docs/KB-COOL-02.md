# Coolant pump seal replacement

Subsystem: cooling
Document ID: KB-COOL-02

A degraded pump seal shows as a slow decline in coolant flow and occasional pressure oscillation. To replace seal kit P-COOL-01: isolate the loop, relieve pressure, drain to the service level, and swap the seal following the torque sequence. Bleed air from the loop before returning to service and confirm flow stabilizes at setpoint. Log the coolant top-up volume for trend tracking.
